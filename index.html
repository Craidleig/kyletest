The Studio

Capturing and
preserving fleeting moments.

We are Kyle and Sophie — a husband-and-wife duo with an eye for bringing your love story to life.

Your wedding day is made up of hundreds of fleeting moments. While you live them, we move quietly through the day to capture and preserve them — cut together into a film you'll return to for the rest of your lives.

Two shooters, two perspectives, one story. It's the reason we work in pairs on every wedding, and the reason we called this Double‑Barrel.
